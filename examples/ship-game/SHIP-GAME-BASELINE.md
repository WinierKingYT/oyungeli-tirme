# Ship Game Discovery Baseline

Status: `DESIGN INPUT — NOT ARCHITECTURE AUTHORITY`

## Voyage

Players depart port A, visit roughly three to four islands, and arrive at port B. Cargo may originate at the first port, be bought/sold at islands, or be partially delivered along the route.

## Cargo

Fragile, flammable, and heavy cargo uses readable physical placement. Weight distribution, securing/strapping, loading/unloading, labels, damage, and limited stock create decisions. Heavy items should matter to ship balance without making the game an opaque simulation.

## Ship and crises

Engine heat can force slowdown. Antenna/radio failures degrade communication or information. Compass/navigation fallback and telegraph-like communication preserve play when advanced systems fail. Crises may chain; unresolved problems can persist with legible consequences rather than requiring every problem to be solved.

## Islands and economy

Islands may offer repair, fuel, fishing, market goods, or no service. Stock and price differences support buy-low/sell-high fallback when missions are weak. Fish and local goods may be traded.

## Navigation and information

Lighthouses matter at night. The captain's desk owns the map. Heat, cargo labels, and status are shown near their physical systems. Persistent HUD is minimal; the default crosshair is a small dot.

## Port stop

Approach -> moor -> inspect market/services -> move freely -> load/unload -> repair/refuel -> secure cargo -> depart.

## Quiet time

Fishing and small optional tasks reduce travel boredom without replacing the voyage loop.

## Character/art direction

One simple common crew body supports cosmetics. Compact/chunky proportions, readable large hands/head, short legs, simple materials, slightly dark stylized world, and low production complexity are preferred. Role identity comes from player action and cosmetics rather than different base bodies.

## Discovery warnings

Engine version, networking model, existing interaction system, physics approach, save/economy ownership, asset budgets, target platforms, and repository reality are unknown. No item above authorizes implementation.
