name: postmortem
description: Produce a blameless postmortem for a serious defect, escaped regression, or failed milestone.
disable-model-invocation: true
allowed-tools: Read Grep Glob
---

For incident `$ARGUMENTS`, establish timeline, impact, detection, response, technical cause, contributing conditions, why controls missed it, recovery, and recurrence-prevention actions.

Separate evidence from inference. Do not name a person as root cause. Create actions with owner, due condition, validation, and closure evidence. Update the anti-pattern or process only when the failure demonstrates a reusable lesson.
