# Game Identity — Ship game (working title)

> One page. Every design decision is checked against this file. Drawn from `docs/01-design/` and `examples/ship-game/SHIP-GAME-BASELINE.md` on 2026-09-25. Items marked **(owner?)** are proposals waiting for the owner's confirmation.

## Vision (one sentence)
A crew of friends sails a working cargo ship from port to port through a chain of islands, keeping cargo, engine, and navigation alive while the sea and their own mistakes create the stories.

## Player fantasy
"We are a scrappy crew who keep an old ship running and still make the delivery."

## Fantasy / Loop / Twist
| | One line |
|---|---|
| Fantasy | a small co-op crew running and repairing a cargo ship |
| Core loop | choose route/cargo → load and secure → sail, navigate, maintain → react to crises → trade/repair at islands → deliver → upgrade |
| Twist | cargo is physical: how you place and secure it changes how the ship behaves in bad weather (owner?) |

## Genre and stage
Co-op physics/management voyage game (1–4 players, owner?) · Stage: **idea** (no prototype yet).

## Pillars
| Pillar | Means | Does NOT mean |
|---|---|---|
| Physical cooperation | handling, securing, repairing, navigating through readable physical actions | menus doing the work; one player able to do everything alone efficiently |
| Emergent voyages | cargo, weather, failures, markets, partial recovery create different stories | scripted events on a timer |
| Readable ship world | information shown near the system that owns it (heat at the engine, labels on cargo); minimal HUD, dot crosshair | spreadsheet UIs or a permanent HUD |
| Meaningful imperfection | not every crisis must be solved; consequences stay legible and recoverable | instant fail states or softlocks |
| Simple production language | stylized, chunky, performant assets; one crew body with cosmetics | realistic art or many unique characters |

## Target player
Friends playing together in 30–60 minute voyages (owner?) · PC first (owner?) · also playable solo with more pressure (owner?).

## Non-goals
- Constant combat as the main source of tension.
- A permanent HUD or full-screen economy spreadsheets.
- An opaque, realistic ship simulation the player cannot predict.

## Tone and art direction
Slightly dark stylized sea world; compact characters with large hands and heads, short legs; simple materials; lighthouses matter at night.

## Critical systems (always full review)
- Save format · Networking/co-op authority · Economy (prices, rewards)
